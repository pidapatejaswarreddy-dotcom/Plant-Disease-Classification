How it works

  1. UploadChoose a clear JPG or PNG photo of one leaf.
  2. CheckThe file type, size and contents are validated.
  3. PreprocessThe image is resized to 224×224 exactly as during training.
  4. ClassifyMobileNetV2 outputs a score for every class it was trained on.
  5. ReviewYou see the top class, its score and cautious general information.
