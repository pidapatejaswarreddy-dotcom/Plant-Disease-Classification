About this project

Objective

Classify plant leaf images into healthy / diseased classes using a convolutional neural network trained with transfer learning, and present the result responsibly in a simple web interface.

Dataset: PlantVillage

Hughes & Salathé (2015) and Mohanty, Hughes & Salathé (2016): colour images of healthy and diseased leaves, one folder per crop/disease class.

Model

MobileNetV2 (ImageNet pre-trained convolutional base) + global average pooling + dropout + softmax layer. Inputs are resized to 224×224; the rescale to [−1, 1] is part of the saved model. Data augmentation (flip, rotation, zoom, contrast) is applied during training only.

Limitations

  • Only the trained classes can be predicted; other plants, diseases or non-leaf images will still receive a (wrong) class unless the score is low.
  • PlantVillage photos are taken under controlled conditions; accuracy on real field photos is usually lower.
  • Scores are uncalibrated softmax outputs, not probabilities of being correct.
  • The guidance shown is general and educational. It is not a treatment recommendation.
